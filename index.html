about

Who I Am

I'm a software engineer based in Abuja, Nigeria, focused on AI agents and LLM-powered automation. I hold a B.Sc. in Cybersecurity from the Air Force Institute of Technology, and I build AI systems that are secure by design, not as an afterthought.

I've shipped AI systems: an n8n invoice pipeline, an operations reporting system, a proposal engine, a content research and publishing agent, a lead research agent built on the Claude Agent SDK, and a voice support agent that reaches its data through a custom MCP server. Each one has a human approval gate enforced in the database, spend limits that hold even if the model misbehaves, and Claude output checked against what the system actually calculated.

I'm drawn to the intersection of engineering rigour and adversarial thinking. That shows up in my AI work too: prompt-injection guards, grounding checks, and agents that have no tool for the things they must never do.

What I've Built

AI Agents & Automation

RelayPay Support Agent — Voice AI Customer Support

A voice support agent that customers can call from the browser or by phone. Vapi handles speech, and a Claude Agent SDK session runs the whole call. The agent answers from an approved knowledge base, looks up accounts, transactions and payouts, opens tickets, books callbacks on a support agent's Google Calendar, and hands anything that needs human judgement to a specialist. Its only way to reach data is a custom MCP server with eight tools, and every turn, tool call and decision is logged to Supabase for review in a role-based support console.

Lead Agent

An autonomous research agent built on the Claude Agent SDK. It turns a qualification objective into ICP criteria, finds companies through Apify, reads their websites with Firecrawl, and qualifies each one from evidence. For each qualified lead it drafts a 3-step cold email sequence and a LinkedIn message for human review. It never sends anything, because no tool in the session can.
